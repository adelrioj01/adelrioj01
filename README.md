# Alberto del Río Jara

I work with data, analytics, and applied AI at the University of Arkansas. I am currently an AI Innovation Analyst Intern and a Graduate Assistant in Strategic Data and Process Innovation while completing an M.S. in Statistics and Analytics with a concentration in Computational Analytics.

My background is in Telecommunication Technologies Engineering at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. That path took me from embedded C and vibration signals to machine learning, cloud data pipelines, and business intelligence.

## Selected work

### [Housing availability analytics pipeline](https://github.com/adelrioj01/End-to-End-Cloud-Data-Pipeline-for-Housing-Availability-Analytics)

A 13-task Airflow pipeline that loads housing data into GCP and builds 10 dbt models for a Looker Studio dashboard. The project includes Terraform infrastructure, GitHub Actions validation, and 61 dbt tests for schema and housing-allocation rules.

`GCP` `BigQuery` `Airflow` `dbt` `Terraform` `Docker` `Looker Studio`

### [Fault diagnosis in rotary machines](https://github.com/adelrioj01/ML-Classifier-for-Fault-Diagnosis-in-Rotary-Machines)

My UC3M bachelor's thesis: an experimental pipeline for detecting and classifying rotary-machine faults from vibration measurements at 25, 50, and 75 RPM. It compares time-domain and FFT features across five statistical and machine-learning models. The thesis received a final grade of 10/10.

`Python` `SciPy` `scikit-learn` `signal processing` `predictive maintenance`
